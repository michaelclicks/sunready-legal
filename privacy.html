SunReady ("SunReady," "we," "us," or "our") is a tanning and UV-index coaching app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. We built SunReady to be privacy-first: the large majority of your data stays on your device.

The short version: Your photos, skin profile, and tanning history are stored locally on your iPhone — we do not upload or sell them. Your location is used to fetch UV and weather data and is not stored on our servers. Subscriptions are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. SunReady provides estimates for educational purposes and is not medical advice.

1. Information we handle

Location. With your permission, SunReady uses your device location to retrieve the local UV index and weather forecast and to suggest tanning times. Location is processed to fetch this data and is not collected or retained on our servers. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather (WeatherKit).

Photos and skin-tone scan. If you add progress photos or complete the optional selfie "skin scan," those images are stored locally on your device. The skin-tone analysis runs on your device; selfie images used for the scan are not uploaded to us or any third party.

Profile and activity you enter. Information such as your skin type, tanning goals, experience level, sunscreen (SPF) choices, and logged tanning sessions is stored locally on your device to personalize coaching and estimates.

Purchase and subscription data. When you subscribe, the transaction is handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status and entitlements. RevenueCat may receive a randomized app user identifier and purchase information to tell the app whether your subscription is active. We do not receive your full payment card details.

App analytics and session recordings. SunReady uses PostHog (EU-hosted) to understand how the app is used, so we can fix what is confusing and improve the coaching. Two kinds of data are involved:

  • Usage events — which screens you open and which actions you take (for example: finishing onboarding, starting a tanning session, opening the subscription screen), together with a randomly generated identifier for your app installation, your device model, OS version and app version. This identifier is not your name, e-mail, Apple ID or advertising ID, and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Session recordings. PostHog records a screen-by-screen replay of how the app is used, so we can see where people get stuck. Images are masked: your progress photos, your skin-scan selfie and the live camera preview are blocked out and never leave your device in a recording. Text shown by the app (UV numbers, coaching lines, plan prices) is visible in the replay; the only text you type in SunReady is a city name in the location search. Recordings are tied to the same anonymous installation identifier, not to your identity.

We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it, and it is never combined with your photos or skin data, which stay on your device. If you would prefer not to be included, e-mail us at the address in section 11 and we will exclude your installation and delete any recordings associated with it.

Diagnostics. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymized crash and usage statistics through App Store Connect if you have enabled sharing in your device settings. This data does not identify you.

2. How we use information

  • To show the local UV index, forecast, and recommended sun-exposure times.
  • To personalize burn-time and Vitamin-D estimates and coaching from "Sunny."
  • To save and display your progress photos and tanning history.
  • To provide and restore your subscription and Pro features.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

3. Third-party services

SunReady relies on a small number of providers to function. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Apple (WeatherKit & App Store) — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • PostHog (product analytics and session replay, EU Cloud) — posthog.com/privacy

We only work with providers that are required, by their policies or our agreements with them, to protect your information to a standard at least equal to the one described in this policy. We do not share user data with third parties that do not provide such protection.

4. Data storage and retention

Your photos, skin profile, and tanning logs are stored on your device. If you delete the app, this local data is removed with it. Subscription records held by Apple and RevenueCat are retained according to their policies.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your photos or skin data with advertisers. We only share the limited information described above with the service providers needed to run the app. Analytics events and session recordings are processed by PostHog on our behalf on servers in the European Union, and are not shared with anyone else.

6. Health disclaimer

SunReady provides UV, burn-time, and Vitamin-D figures as estimates for educational purposes only. They are not medical advice and should not be relied on to prevent sunburn, skin damage, or any health condition. Always follow the guidance of qualified health professionals and applicable sun-safety recommendations.

7. Children

SunReady is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Your choices and rights

You can revoke location or photo permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app. Because your photos and profile are stored locally, you can delete them within the app or by removing the app from your device. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal data; contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by law.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
