Frequently asked questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. To view, change, or cancel, open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → SunReady. You can also restore a previous purchase from the SunReady paywall using Restore.

Why does SunReady need my location?

Your location is used to fetch the local UV index and weather forecast so we can show accurate sun-exposure guidance. You can manage this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Are the burn-time and Vitamin-D numbers medical advice?

No. They are estimates for educational purposes only and are not medical advice. Always use appropriate sun protection and consult a qualified health professional for medical guidance.

What happens to my photos and skin scan?

Your progress photos and skin-tone scan stay on your device and are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes this local data.
